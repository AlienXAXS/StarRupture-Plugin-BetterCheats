- The version object map can switch a section off while the game runs. The section stops and gives back what it changed (weapon, movement and tool values, stack sizes, Unlock All Buildings, enemy AI, the wave pause, machine power). Stats pins, Skills, Corporations, spawned items and the inventory grid size stay as they are. Turn it back on and your saved settings apply again once the world has finished loading. A section that was off when the plugin loaded stays off until a RELOAD
- Item stack sizes are now a section of their own, separate from the inventory grid size and Plant Pickup. If the version map switches them off, the page says so and every item goes back to its own cap, and your saved sizes apply again when they come back on. Otherwise nothing changes
- You can now install and update it from the ModLoader's Browse list.

- Item Stack Sizes: the sliders are gone. Each item shows its default stack size; type a new size and press Enter, or pick x2, x5 or x10 of the default. The all-items and category rows work the same way, and each row shows what is set
- Stack size presets keep the multiples. Presets saved by earlier versions still load, and a preset that can't be read now says so and leaves your settings alone. An earlier version reads a multiple on the all-items or a category row as not set
- Fixed: Only show changed in Item Stack Sizes no longer lists items that can't stack
- Fixed: the Fill, Clear and Full Heal buttons on the Self page are fully visible
- The version object map can switch a section off while the game runs. The section stops and gives back what it changed (weapon, movement and tool values, stack sizes, Unlock All Buildings, enemy AI, the wave pause, machine power). Stats pins, Skills, Corporations, spawned items and the inventory grid size stay as they are. Turn it back on and your saved settings apply again once the world has finished loading. A section that was off when the plugin loaded stays off until a RELOAD
- Item stack sizes are now a section of their own, separate from the inventory grid size and Plant Pickup. If the version map switches them off, the page says so and every item goes back to its own cap, and your saved sizes apply again when they come back on. Otherwise nothing changes
- Fixed: a changed magazine size, grenade charge cost or grenade damage could carry over into another save or server, and a changed machine power value could stick after a RELOAD
- Fixed: if control briefly left your character with Fly mode on, you could come back still passing through walls or with the wrong flight speed, even after turning Fly off
- When you join someone else's game, your own settings for weapon Damage and Fire Rate, No Handheld Drill Overheat, Mining Boost, Plant Pickup, No Build Cost, No Stability Check and Unlock All Buildings no longer apply there, because nothing shows they take effect from your own game. Those rows are greyed out. Your saved settings are kept and still work in your own game
- You can now install and update it from the ModLoader's Browse list.
